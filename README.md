# 🏮 Lantern Alley

A cozy, offline-first street food night market incremental game designed for one-thumb mobile play — whether you're killing five minutes or settling into a long flight.

**Play Live:** https://b-rob32.github.io/lantern-alley/

## ✈️ Play Offline on Your Phone (Airplane Mode Ready)

Lantern Alley is an installable Progressive Web App (PWA) with a precaching Service Worker. Once installed, it runs fullscreen with **zero internet connection** required:

- **iPhone / iPad (Safari):** Open the link above, tap the **Share** button, and choose **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** Open the link above, tap the menu (`⋮`), and choose **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**.

## ✨ Highlights

- **2-Tier Cozy Supply Chains:** Keep Prep Stations (*Charcoal, Broth, Dough, Tea, Sweet Paste*) stocked to feed Market Stalls (*Yakitori, Midnight Ramen, Soup Dumplings, Brown Sugar Boba, Warm Taiyaki, Imperial Night Feast*), with gentle bottleneck hints and 1-tap prep shortcuts.
- **Animated Cozy Dusk Alley:** Watch swaying paper lanterns, rising broth steam, strolling patrons, and glowing stalls come alive on a retina HTML5 Canvas — plus tap drifting **Golden Lanterns** for 2x Night Rush bursts.
- **Patron Cravings & Regulars:** Serve rotating cravings to earn coin bursts and befriend charming night-market regulars in your journal.
- **Grand Night Festival Prestige & 4 Districts:** Host festivals to earn permanent Festival Lanterns, unlock Heirloom Recipes, and expand across **Lantern Alley**, **Harbor Pier**, **Blossom Courtyard**, and **Neon Rooftop**.
- **Plane-Friendly QoL:** Welcome-back offline earnings receipt, 1-tap Base64 save backup/restore, `1x / 10x / Max` bulk buying, muted-by-default synthesized Web Audio SFX, and subtle haptics.
